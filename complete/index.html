Brown Butter Zucchini Bread

A little sweet, a little savory.

This moist zucchini bread is made with brown butter, white miso, and dark chocolate. The miso adds saltiness and umami, while the brown butter gives the bread a rich,nutty flavor.

Prep time

25 minutes

Cook time

Baking Instructions

  1. Prep the zucchini: Grate the zucchini and gently squeeze out some of the excess water.
  2. Make the brown miso butter: Melt the butter over medium heat. Add the miso and mix into a paste. Let it bubble, foam, and turn golden brown for about 4–5 minutes.
  3. Combine with zucchini: Pour the hot brown butter mixture over the grated zucchini and whisk.
  4. Add the wet ingredients: Add the sugar, eggs, and vanilla. Whisk until smooth.
  5. Add the dry ingredients: Stir in the flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
  6. Fold in chocolate: Gently mix in the chopped chocolate.
  7. Bake: Pour the batter into a parchment-lined 9x5-inch loaf pan. Bake at 350°F for 50–55 minutes.
  8. Cool: Let the bread rest for 30–45 minutes before slicing.
